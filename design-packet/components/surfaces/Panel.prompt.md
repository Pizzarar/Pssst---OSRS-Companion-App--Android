Raised stone panel (gradient + 1px bevel) — the container for every content block in the app and docs.
```jsx
<Panel padding={14}><h3>Total Account Wealth</h3></Panel>
```
- `padding`: 14 for header/summary panels, 12 for list cards, 10 for tight rows, 4 for tab bars.
- `radius`: 6 (default). Never add drop shadows.
