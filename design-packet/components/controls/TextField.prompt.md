Outlined text field with floating label; gold 2px border on focus, red helper text for warnings.
```jsx
<TextField label="Server URL" placeholder="http://192.168.1.20:8788" supportingText="127.0.0.1 is this phone, not your PC. Use your PC's address, such as http://192.168.1.20:8788" />
```
- In the app, supporting text is only ever a warning/error, so it renders red.
